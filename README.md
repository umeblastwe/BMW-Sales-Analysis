# 🚗 BMW Car Sales Analysis (2010–2024) - Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing global BMW car sales, revenue performance, pricing trends, fuel types, transmission preferences, and regional market behaviors from 2010 to 2024. The project features an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard built using advanced data modeling and DAX measures.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Overview Page
* **Key Metrics:** Total Sales Volume (**253M**), Total Revenue (**19T**), Average Revenue, and Average Mileage (**100.31K KM**)[cite: 7].
* **Visual Breakdown:** Regional distribution shows balanced sales volume across all major global regions (Asia, Europe, North America, Middle East, Africa, and South America)[cite: 7]. 
* **Fuel Type Distribution:** Evenly split among Hybrid (**25.47%** / 65M), Petrol (**24.99%** / 63M), Electric (**24.93%** / 63M), and Diesel (**24.61%** / 62M)[cite: 7].
* **Top Models:** Visualized via a treemap highlighting key models like 7 Series, 3 Series, M5, X3, X5, X1, i8, and i3[cite: 7].

### 2. Regional Analysis
* **Market Leader:** **Asia** leads the global market with **16.96%** of total sales, where the **X1** model holds top popularity[cite: 6].
* **Regional Preferences:** 
  * **Middle East:** Dominated by the **X5** model[cite: 6].
  * **North America:** Strong preference for the **X1** model[cite: 6].
  * **Europe:** Leans more toward the **M5** model[cite: 6].
  * **Africa & South America:** **X1** leads in Africa, while the **X6** stands out as the top seller in South America[cite: 6].

### 3. Yearly Analysis
* **COVID-19 Impact:** A noticeable dip in sales and revenue occurred in **2020** due to global market disruptions[cite: 5].
* **2022 Surge:** A significant spike in sales driven heavily by increased demand for **hybrid cars**[cite: 5]. Interestingly, average price per unit dropped in 2022 due to higher sales of mid-range hybrid models rather than premium variants[cite: 5].
* **2023 Slowdown:** Revenue dipped in 2023, reflecting a slowdown in sales volume or a shift toward more affordable models[cite: 5].

### 4. Car Model Analysis
* **Top Performer:** **BMW 7 Series** leads in sales, driven by its popularity as a hybrid model[cite: 4].
* **Model Insights:** The **M3** lags behind due to its non-hybrid, gasoline engine[cite: 4].
* **Pricing Trend:** 2015 recorded the lowest average prices, followed by a sharp increase in 2016, after which prices stabilized[cite: 4].

### 5. Fuel Type & Mileage Analysis
* **Pricing:** **Electric cars** command the highest average price, followed by diesel, petrol, and hybrid cars[cite: 3].
* **Mileage & Value:** Petrol cars offer the lowest mileage, while **diesel cars offer the highest mileage**[cite: 3]. Hybrid cars provide great mileage at a lower average price, making them the best value-for-money option[cite: 3].

### 6. Manual & Automatic Analysis
* **Transmission Split:** Sales are nearly equally split between automatic (**50.27%** / 127M) and manual (**49.73%** / 126M)[cite: 2].
* **Trends:** Manual car sales dropped sharply in **2020** but rebounded strongly in **2021 and 2022**[cite: 2]. The overall sales gap between manual and automatic is minimal (~0.54%), showing equal buyer preference[cite: 2].
